From a posted requirement to a recorded payment.

Eight stages. At each one you can see who acts, and what the company and the professional see.

1

Post a requirement

A five-step form: basic details, scope and location, deliverables with acceptance criteria, budget, then review. Progress is saved as a draft.

CompanySets the brief and the budget
ProfessionalNot involved yet
2

Worklens reviews it

An admin reads the requirement. Clear ones are published. Unclear ones come back with comments to fix. Insurance requirements answer a short compliance declaration first.

CompanyEdits and resubmits if asked
ProfessionalNot involved yet
3

Professionals apply or are invited

Verified professionals whose industry, skills and location match can see it, without the company name. They apply at the stated budget or bid. Worklens can also invite someone directly.

CompanyWaits; sees that the requirement is open
ProfessionalApplies, bids, declines or asks a question
4

Worklens selects, the professional confirms

An admin selects one professional. They confirm and acknowledge confidentiality. Only now are the company and the professional named to each other.

CompanySees who has been assigned
ProfessionalConfirms the assignment
5

Work is done and submitted

The professional carries out the work and submits text, photographs and documents from the web or the mobile app.

CompanyIs notified that work is ready
ProfessionalSubmits the deliverable
6

The company reviews

Approve, ask for a correction with comments, or reject with a reason. Corrections can repeat up to a set limit; after that Worklens steps in.

CompanyApproves, corrects or rejects
ProfessionalResubmits if asked
7

Worklens gives final approval

An admin checks the approved work once more. The assignment cannot be completed without this step.

CompanyWaits for completion
ProfessionalWaits for completion
8

Invoice and payment

The invoice is generated with the service fee, GST and payout shown. Payment happens by bank transfer outside the platform; Worklens records it, and the professional sees the payout in their earnings.

CompanyDownloads the invoice and pays
ProfessionalSees gross, TDS and net payable

The exceptions are part of the design.

When things do not go to plan, each case has a clear owner and a next step.

Nobody accepts in time

The requirement is marked expired and an admin extends or closes it.

A professional drops out

An admin reassigns the work to another verified professional and records the reason.

A question about the brief

The professional asks Worklens, who answers or checks with the company, without sharing names.

The review is late

Reminders go out. After the review period the case moves to an admin, who can act for the company.

A dispute

Either side can raise one on a live assignment, or within 48 hours of completion. An admin decides.

An account is suspended

The user cannot log in, and any live assignments are flagged for an admin to handle.
